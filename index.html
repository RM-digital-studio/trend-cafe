Our Story

Welcome to Trend Cafe, Skopje's newest destination for coffee aficionados and pastry lovers. We believe in crafting exceptional experiences, one cup and one bite at a time. Our carefully sourced specialty espresso beans are expertly brewed to perfection, offering a rich and aromatic journey.

Indulge in our selection of freshly baked croissants, flaky and golden, perfect with your morning coffee. For those special occasions, our skilled bakers create stunning custom cakes that taste as incredible as they look. Enjoy the warm atmosphere indoors or relax in our cozy outdoor seating area, a perfect spot to unwind and savor the moment.
