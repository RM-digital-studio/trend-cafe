Our Story

Welcome to Trend Cafe, Skopje's newest destination for exquisite coffee and delightful pastries. Nestled in a cozy spot, we pride ourselves on serving only the finest specialty espresso, expertly crafted by our skilled baristas.

From flaky, buttery croissants baked fresh daily to custom-designed cakes for every occasion, our bakery delights are made with passion and the highest quality ingredients. Enjoy our offerings in our warm, inviting interior or soak up the sun in our charming outdoor seating area.
